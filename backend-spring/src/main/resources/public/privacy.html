Privacy notice

Brief! · in force from October 2026

In short: Brief! keeps what it needs to run your account and show your posts, and nothing else. No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data. Your email address is seen by you alone. Delete your account and it is gone, with your posts.

Who is responsible

OneTechly, LLC, New York, United States, which runs Brief! (briefmessage.net). Contact: support@briefmessage.net.

What Brief! keeps, and why

What Why Who can see it
Your username To show who wrote a post, and to sign you in The members of the board
Your email address, and whether it is confirmed To confirm it is yours, and to send you a link if you forget your password. Nothing else: no newsletters. You alone
Your password To sign you in. Stored only as a salted hash, which cannot be turned back into the password. Nobody
Your posts, when they were written, and who they mention That is the service The people who can read the board
Invitations: the address an invitation was sent to, and whether it was used To let invited people join a closed board. An invitation nobody used is deleted once it has been expired for 30 days, the next time the board's invitations are opened or sent. The person who sent the invitation
Addresses that mail could not be delivered to, the date, and the reason the receiving server gave So that Brief! can tell you your link did not arrive, and does not mail a refused address again. Deleted after 30 days, or earlier when the address is confirmed, tried again, or its account is deleted. You, and the person who invited you if it was an invitation
Your sign-ins: one per device, with a short name for it such as "Chrome on Windows" (not the full browser description), when it started and when it was last used To keep you signed in, and to let you see where you are signed in and sign any device out. Deleted when you sign out, set a new password or delete your account. A sign-in that ends by itself (after 12 hours, or 30 days without use if you chose "Stay signed in") stops working at once and is deleted soon after. You alone
Notifications: for each device where you turned them on (possible only where you chose "Stay signed in"), the address your browser's push service gave it, and the keys that encrypt notifications for it To tell you when someone mentions you. A notification holds who mentioned you and the start of the post, encrypted so that only your browser can read it. Deleted when you turn notifications off, sign that device out or delete your account, and when that device's sign-in ends: a device you are no longer signed in on receives nothing. Nobody; your browser's push service delivers it without being able to read it
Your network (IP) address To slow down someone guessing passwords or invitation links. Kept only in the running server's memory, never in the database, and forgotten when the server restarts. Nobody; see also "Services we use"

Brief! sets no cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in and a few settings (such as "Stay signed in" and which mentions you have read) in its own storage on your device, where only Brief! can read them. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Services we use

Brief! runs on three services that handle data for us, and for no other purpose:

  • Render (Render Services, Inc., United States) hosts the application and its database. Its logs record visits, including network addresses.
  • Resend (United States) delivers the emails Brief! sends: confirmations, password links and invitations. It tells Brief! when one cannot be delivered.
  • Cloudflare (United States) provides the domain name and forwards mail sent to support@briefmessage.net.

If you turn on notifications, they travel through the push service of your browser's maker: Google for Chrome and for Edge on Android, Mozilla for Firefox, Apple for Safari, Microsoft for Edge on Windows. Brief! chooses none of these; your browser does. Each notification is encrypted for your browser alone, so the push service sees only that a notification for your device arrived, and when.

Each keeps data only as long as its own service needs. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

How long

Your account and posts are kept until you delete them. When you delete your account, it is removed from the database at once, together with your posts, your email address, the invitation you joined with, any links mailed to you, your sign-ins and your notification settings. If the hosting provider keeps backups of the database, deleted data leaves those backups when they expire, within days.

Your choices

  • See what Brief! holds about you under Your account.
  • Correct your email address while it is not confirmed yet; for anything else, write to support.
  • Delete your posts one by one, or your whole account, whenever you want.
  • Ask us any question about your data at support@briefmessage.net.

Children

Brief! is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep data about them. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version appears here with a new date, and members are told about important changes before they apply.
