Pricing

Brief! is free during early access. What that means, and what happens later.

Free during early access. Every feature, for every member. No card is asked for.

What early access means

Brief! is new and opens step by step, by invitation. While it is in early access, using it costs nothing, and members help shape what comes next.

When paid plans arrive

  • Paid plans will be announced on this page, and to members by email, at least 30 days before any of them starts.
  • Nothing will ever be charged without you choosing a plan and entering a payment method yourself.
  • The refund policy for paid plans will be published before the first charge.
