What Brief! is

A message board where every post fits in 255 characters, so every post gets read.

Brief! is a message board for a group of people who work or meet together. Members write short posts that everyone on the board reads. There is one rule that shapes everything else: a post fits in 255 characters, about two or three sentences.

Why 255 characters

Long messages get skimmed, saved for later, and then forgotten. A limit makes the writer put the point first: what happened, what is needed, by whom. The ruler under the post box shows how much room is left as you type, so the limit guides you instead of cutting you off. The result is a board that can be read from top to bottom in a minute.

What you can do

  • Post to everyone on the board, and edit or delete your own posts later.
  • Mention a member by writing their name, with or without an @. They see it under Mentions, with a count in the browser tab, and a desktop alert while Brief! is open if they allow alerts.
  • Share links: web addresses in a post become clickable.
  • Install it on a phone or computer from the browser, with Install Brief! on this device. No app store is involved.

Built to be dependable

Brief! keeps your posts in a managed PostgreSQL database and serves the site over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Every release must pass a full suite of automated tests before it goes live.

Who runs it

Brief! is made by OneTechly, LLC in New York, the company behind the PixelPerfect screenshot API. Today it is in early access: briefmessage.net runs one board, and new members join by invitation.

Still have a question? Write to support@briefmessage.net. A person reads every message.
