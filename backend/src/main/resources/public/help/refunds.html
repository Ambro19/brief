Refund policy

How refunds work, today and once paid plans begin.

Brief! charges nothing today, so there is nothing to refund.

Before paid plans start

When paid plans are introduced, the refund terms for them will be set out on this page before the first charge, together with how to cancel. They will apply only to payments made after they are published.

A charge you did not expect

If you are ever charged by OneTechly, LLC for Brief! by mistake, write to support@briefmessage.net with the date and amount, and the charge will be refunded.

Still have a question? Write to support@briefmessage.net. A person reads every message.
