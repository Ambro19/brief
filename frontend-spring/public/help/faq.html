Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions members ask most.

Is Brief! free?

Yes, during early access. See Pricing.

How do I join?

With an invitation from the person who runs the board. See Getting started.

My link says it has expired. What now?

Each link lasts a limited time: an invitation 7 days, an email confirmation 24 hours, a password link one hour. Each works once. For a confirmation, sign in and choose Send the link again; for a password, ask for a new link; for an invitation, ask the person who invited you.

Why can't I post?

Your email address is waiting to be confirmed. Open the link Brief! mailed you, or send it again from the box at the top.

I did not receive an email.

Mail comes from hello@briefmessage.net. Look in spam and in tabs such as Updates or Promotions, or search your mail for Brief!. Then send the link again.

Brief! says it couldn't deliver my link.

The receiving mail server refused it, almost always because of a typing mistake: one letter missing or added is enough, and the mail then goes to an address that does not exist. Check the address shown on the page letter by letter, enter the right one, and a new link is sent at once. For a day Brief! does not mail the refused address again, because the mail would only be refused again; if you are sure the address was right, you can try it once more after that.

I was invited at an address that already has an account.

Each address belongs to one account. If the invited address already has an account on the board, the invitation cannot create a second one: sign in with that account instead. If you meant to join with a new account, ask for an invitation to a different address. The person who invited you sees such an invitation as Already a member.

How do I get notified when someone mentions me?

Choose Turn on notifications on the Mentions page or in Your account, and allow them when your browser asks. Each device is turned on separately, and only where you signed in with Stay signed in on this device ticked: a sign-in without it ends when the tab closes, and a shared computer should not keep receiving your mentions. A notification shows who mentioned you and the start of the post, also on a locked screen; tap it to open your mentions. Send a test notification in Your account shows what one looks like. If several mentions arrive at once, the newest replaces the previous one, so your screen does not fill up.

Why can't I turn on notifications on my iPhone?

Apple allows notifications only for web apps added to the Home Screen, on iOS 16.4 or later. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open Brief! from the new icon, sign in, and turn notifications on there. If you once refused notifications, allow them again in the iPhone's Settings, under Notifications, then Brief!. In a browser on a computer, they are allowed again in the site settings (the icon left of the address).

How long do I stay signed in? I lost my phone.

With Stay signed in on this device ticked, 30 days from the last time you used Brief! on that device, and a year at most; without, 12 hours, or until the tab closes. Your account lists where you are signed in. Choose Sign out next to the lost phone, or Sign out everywhere else: the phone is signed out at once, and receives no more notifications. Setting a new password through Forgot password? signs you out everywhere, too.

Who can see my posts and my email address?

Your posts are seen by the members of the board. Your email address is seen by you alone; it is used only to confirm it is yours and to help you back in. Details are in the Privacy notice.

Can I change or delete a post?

Yes. Edit and Delete appear under each of your own posts.

Can I use Brief! on my phone?

Yes. Open briefmessage.net in the phone's browser and choose Install Brief! on this device, or add it to the home screen from the browser's menu.

How do I delete my account?

Open Your account and choose Delete my account. It asks for your password, and deletes the account and every post you wrote, at once. It cannot be undone.

How do I reach you?

Write to support@briefmessage.net.

Still have a question? Write to support@briefmessage.net. A person reads every message.
