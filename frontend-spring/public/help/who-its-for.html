Who Brief! is for

Teams and groups that need to keep each other informed without another long thread.

Brief! suits any group that shares a stream of short updates and wants everyone to actually read them. A few examples:

Software teams

Stand-up notes, "deploying at 3 PM", "the staging database is back", who is on call this week. A mention reaches the one person who needs to act; everyone else stays informed without a meeting.

Small businesses

Shift handovers, a supplier who is running late, today's special, a reminder before an inspection. Short enough to read between two customers, and still there for whoever comes in next.

Project groups, classes and clubs

Deadlines, changes of room or time, who brings what. The whole history stays on one page, in order, and new members can catch up in minutes.

When Brief! is not the right tool

Long documents, private conversations between two people and file sharing belong elsewhere. Brief! does one thing: short updates to the whole group. It works best next to the tools you already use, not instead of them.

What is available today

During early access, briefmessage.net runs one invitation-only board. Separate private boards for each team, run by their own owner, are planned. If your team would like to try Brief!, write to support@briefmessage.net.

Still have a question? Write to support@briefmessage.net. A person reads every message.
