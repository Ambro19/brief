Getting started

Joining with an invitation, confirming your address, posting, mentioning people and notifications.

1. Join with an invitation

The board is invitation-only. The person who runs it sends you an invitation by email, or a link by message. Opening it shows “… invited you to Brief!” with the form to create your account. An invitation works once, for 7 days; if yours has expired, ask for a new one.

2. Choose a username and a password

Your username is how other members see and mention you: 3 to 30 letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores. Your password needs at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number.

3. Confirm your email address

If the invitation came by email, your address is already confirmed and you can post at once. If you joined with a link, Brief! mails you a confirmation link: until you open it you can read everything, but not post. The link works once, for 24 hours. If it does not arrive, look in the spam folder or in tabs such as Updates, then use Send the link again. Type the address carefully: if mail to it is refused, Brief! tells you so on the page and asks for the address again.

4. Post and mention

Write in the box at the top, and post with the button or Ctrl + Enter. To get someone's attention, write their username in the post. Your own mentions are under Mentions; the count also appears in the browser tab.

5. Stay signed in, or not

Tick Stay signed in on this device only on a device that is yours: you then stay signed in for 30 days from the last time you used Brief! there (at most a year), so a device you use every week never asks again. Notifications need it. On a shared computer, leave it unticked (the sign-in then lasts 12 hours at most) and use Sign out when you are done. Your account lists every device you are signed in on, and can sign any of them out. Forgot your password? Use Forgot password?: Brief! mails a link that works once, for one hour, and signs you out everywhere once the new password is set.

6. Get notified when someone mentions you

Open Mentions or Your account and choose Turn on notifications, then allow them when the browser asks. From then on this device shows a notification when someone mentions you, even when Brief! is closed. Turn them on on each device where you want them; it needs Stay signed in on this device, ticked when you signed in. On iPhone and iPad, install Brief! first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and turn them on in the installed app.

Still have a question? Write to support@briefmessage.net. A person reads every message.
